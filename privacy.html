Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This website

doctorasma.com is a simple information page for the Doctor Asma YouTube channel. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics, and it does not collect personal information. Video thumbnails on the page are loaded from YouTube (i.ytimg.com), which may log your visit under Google's Privacy Policy.

Doctor Asma Uploads (our YouTube upload tool)

"Doctor Asma Uploads" is a private tool that runs only on our own computer. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload our own videos to our own channel (youtube.com/@DoctorAsma) and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, subtitles, playlists and publishing schedule. It is used only by the channel owner and her family; it has no other users.

  • What it accesses: only our own channel, through the permission the channel owner grants on Google's sign-in page. It does not read or store any other user's data.
  • What it stores: the sign-in permission (an access token) is kept only on our own computer. It keeps a local list of the videos it uploaded (title, date and link). Nothing is sent to anyone other than Google/YouTube.
  • Sharing: we do not sell or share any data.
  • Removing access: the permission can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

By using the tool, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
